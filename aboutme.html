About Me

Hi, I'm Ayman Al Islam — a Data Analyst and BI professional based in Canada,
blending technical expertise with real-world business experience to turn data into decisions.

  • View Projects

How I Started

My path to data analysis wasn't a straight line — and I think that's what makes my perspective unique. I grew up in Saudi Arabia with a passion for engineering, which led me to pursue a foundation degree in Industrial Engineering at the International Islamic University of Malaysia.

My Journey into Data

Over time, my curiosity pulled me toward technology and deeper problem-solving, which led me to make a bold move: relocating to Canada to complete a Diploma in Computer Programming. There I built a strong technical foundation in C, C++, Python, SQL, and data structures. But it was data analysis that truly clicked. There's something deeply satisfying about taking a messy dataset and transforming it into a clear, actionable story — and that's when I went deeper into SQL, Excel, and Tableau. Today I bring an engineer's structured thinking and a programmer's technical fluency to every data challenge I tackle.

Real-World Business Experience

While pursuing my academic goals, I joined Dave's Hot Chicken to support myself — and what started as a practical decision turned into a valuable chapter of professional growth. Working in a high-volume, fast-paced environment sharpened my ability to stay accurate, organized, and composed under pressure. Within a year, I was promoted to Team Leader, where I took on responsibility for staff coordination, customer conflict resolution, weekly inventory counts, supply ordering, and labour management. These experiences gave me a ground-level understanding of how businesses actually run — something I carry directly into my work with data.

Where Operations Met Analytics

Driven by my growing interest in data analysis, I volunteered to take on a Management Trainee role under my store manager. This hands-on experience bridged the gap between business operations and data work. I learned to operate CRM tools, analyze sales, supply, and staffing data, write structured reports, and build dashboards that supported management decisions. This role confirmed what I already suspected: I thrive at the intersection of data and real business problems.

What I Bring to Your Team

Whether the role involves analyzing data, maintaining databases, or ensuring information is accurate and well-organized, I show up with the same mindset: methodical, thorough, and focused on getting it right. I understand how data flows through a business because I've worked in one. I know what managers need to see in a report because I've built them. And I know the cost of inaccurate data because I've managed inventory and operations firsthand.
